# Better Me

A private, single-user system that optimizes one thing: my daily energy and performance.

It ingests my own Oura Ring biometrics, asks me two short questions a day by email
(the variables Oura is blind to: training, food, alcohol, caffeine, stress), and
cross-references the two to find what actually moves my energy.

Not a dashboard. Not a product. Not distributed to anyone else.

Under construction.
