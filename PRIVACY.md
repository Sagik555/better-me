# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

## Who this covers

**Better Me** is a private application built and operated by a single individual for that
individual's own exclusive use. It has no other users, no sign-ups, and no public interface.
It is not offered as a service to anyone.

## What data is processed

- **Oura Ring data** belonging to the sole operator, retrieved from the Oura API v2 under an
  OAuth2 authorization granted by that same person to their own application: sleep, activity,
  readiness, stress, resilience, heart rate, workouts, and related daily summaries.
- **Self-reported data** entered by the sole operator by replying to their own emails:
  training, meals, alcohol, caffeine, perceived stress, energy, and free-text notes.

## Where it goes

All data is stored in a private Turso (libSQL) database accessible only to the operator, and
processed on Vercel infrastructure under the operator's own account. Free-text replies are sent
to the Google Gemini API for parsing into structured fields, under Google's API terms.

No data is sold, shared, published, or transmitted to any third party beyond the processors
named above, all of which are engaged solely to run this application for its single user.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as the operator finds it useful and is deleted at will by deleting
the database. Oura access can be revoked at any time from the Oura account settings, which
immediately and permanently ends this application's access to that data.

## Analytics, tracking, advertising

None. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no profiling of any person other
than the sole operator, by their own choice, about themselves.

## Not medical advice

This application produces observations about correlations in one person's own data. It does not
diagnose, treat, or offer medical advice, and it is not a medical device.
